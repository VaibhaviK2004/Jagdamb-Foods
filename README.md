# Jagdamb Foods - Premium Confectionery Storefront (Redesigned)

A state-of-the-art Next.js 14+ storefront for **Jagdamb Foods Pvt. Ltd.** inspired by the royal Indian heritage aesthetic of [The Kataria Foods](https://thekatariafoods.com/).

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Design Transformation

### 1. The Aesthetic Shift (Why the Client Will Love It)
- **Royal Indian Heritage Color Palette**: Deep terracotta crimson (`#C0392B`, `#A42325`), warm ivory cream (`#FAF5EC`, `#FFFDF9`), and warm gold accents (`#D4A373`).
- **Editorial Indian Confectionery Typography**: Headings rendered in Google Fonts **Fraunces** (antique serif with italic flourishes) paired with modern **Jost** geometric body copy.
- **Traditional Motifs & Vintage Watercolor Art**: Artisanal storytelling featuring women artisans and traditional Maharashtrian confectionery making.
- **Kataria-Style Layout Architecture**:
  - Top Announcement Ticker with live wholesale announcements.
  - "The Jagdamb Foods Experience" curated collection cards.
  - Product cards with crimson "Sale" / "Bestseller" badges, strike-through MRP, and offer pricing.
  - Interactive slide-out **Quotation Basket / Cart Drawer**.
  - One-click **WhatsApp Direct Order Generator** that formats selected items, quantities, and customer details into a ready-to-send message to `+91 9309489012`.
  - Floating WhatsApp action button with live pulse animation.
